# 2014 Ford F-150 Lab

## Projects

### Courtesy + sequential running-board LEDs

Desired behavior: white courtesy lighting on unlock or door activation; amber sequential illumination only with left/right turn signaling and hazards; no manual white switch. Use a current-limited, independently powered **bench mockup** first.

1. Determine the LED kit's electrical requirements and controller behavior from its actual instructions.
2. Draw fused accessory power and a separate low-current trigger circuit.
3. Verify trigger characteristics with a wiring diagram and appropriate measurements; do not assume a wire color.
4. Use a rated isolated input/controller or relay interface and keep BCM outputs protected.
5. Validate normal, reverse and hazard signaling in the bench mockup.

### Accessory power distribution

Inventory loads such as stereo, dashcam, footwells and running boards. For each, record current demand, fuse rating, wiring length, switching behavior and connector ratings. Do not install until the complete design is reviewed and verified.

### Read-only OBD-II telemetry

Start with standard supported PIDs such as RPM, coolant temperature and vehicle speed. Avoid modifying ECU programming or sending raw bus control frames.

## What to record

Keep a private worksheet with battery voltage, measured device power consumption, relevant manuals, sketches, fuse specifications and source citations.
