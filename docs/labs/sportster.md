# 1997 Harley-Davidson Sportster 883 Hugger Lab

## Why this motorcycle?

The older Sportster provides a practical model for direct wiring, switches, grounds, relays, stator AC and regulated charging DC.

## Projects

### Auxiliary chrome lightbar

Design a bench mockup for the planned turn signals and optional spotlights. Calculate the expected current draw from rated bulb wattage, choose suitable automotive-rated protection and switching, then confirm available charging capacity using the correct service literature. **Do not assume your motorcycle has unused charging capacity.**

### Stator versus battery

Sketch the flow: mechanical engine motion → AC stator → regulator/rectifier → DC battery → fused loads. Understand AC measurement theory without probing stator circuits until you have the approved service test procedure and a suitable meter.

### CarPlay accessory power

Compare permanent battery feed, switched accessory feed and regulated USB-type supplies. Examine standby draw and safe shutdown behavior.

## Field notes

Record observed resting battery voltage, charging behavior under manufacturer-approved procedures and any ground or connector maintenance. Do not publish VIN or private location data.
