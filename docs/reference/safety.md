# Electrical Safety Rules

1. Begin with low-voltage, current-limited bench supplies and low-power loads.
2. Always check multimeter mode, lead jacks and probe ratings before measuring.
3. Measure **voltage across** two points; current typically requires an **in-series** or correctly rated clamp measurement.
4. Never perform continuity or resistance tests on a powered circuit.
5. Never put a meter in current mode directly across a battery.
6. Never connect Raspberry Pi GPIO directly to 12V vehicle circuits.
7. Avoid airbags/SRS, live ignition high voltage, moving belts, hot components and high-current exposed contacts.
8. Treat modern BCM outputs and CAN networks as sensitive; read-only diagnostic experiments only.
9. Verify year-specific documentation, wire colors, fuses, connector ratings and allowable loads.
10. Protect circuits near their power source and prevent exposed terminals or loose test leads.

A wire's AWG alone does not establish a safe current rating. Insulation, temperature, routing, length, connectors, fuse protection and standards all matter.
